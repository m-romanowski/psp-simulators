What the route does that this wizard does not, and how the payment is recorded

This wizard hands the payment to the same operation a client application reaches on 's own route, and that operation refuses it exactly as it would refuse a call that arrived there. What the wizard does not do is travel that route: the checks the route itself makes on a client's credential and signature are not made on a payment an operator started, and the table above says what the simulator sent in their place. The payment is recorded as started from this screen, and that is the origin the Payments screen and the audit trail report, so a payment you clicked into existence is never reported as one a client sent.
